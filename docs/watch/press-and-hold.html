Press and hold to move apps

Move an app or set its aim right where you see it, on your home screen or in Everything else, and put your home screen in the order you'd like.

The steps, in words
  1. Press and hold an app on your home screen. Its menu lets you move it, set an aim, or open App info.
  2. App info opens Android's own page for the app.
  3. Set an aim for how much time you'd like each day. Once you've passed it, opening the app begins with a short pause.
  4. It works the same in Everything else.
  5. Tap Move to your home screen, and the app is close at hand. Swipe back to your home screen to see it.
  6. Changed your mind? Press and hold to move it back.
  7. Set it aside puts an app out of sight, and its notifications are cleared. Bringing it back will ask for your key.
  8. To rearrange your home screen, press and hold an empty part of it, then tap Rearrange your home screen. It's first in a home screen app's menu, too.
  9. Slide an app by its handle to where you'd like it.
  10. Tap Done when it feels right. Back to the usual order is there whenever you'd like it.

Press and hold to choose where your apps live, and in what order.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
