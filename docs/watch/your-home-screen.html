Organizing your home screen

Choose the few apps your home screen holds, and put them in the order you'd like.

The steps, in words
  1. Your home screen holds the few apps you reach for most.
  2. To add one, press and hold it in Everything else. Then tap Move to your home screen.
  3. Back on your home screen, they join the list. Phone and Messages come first, then the rest by name.
  4. To change the order, press and hold an empty part of the home screen.
  5. Tap Rearrange your home screen. It's first in a home screen app's menu, too.
  6. Slide an app by its handle to where you'd like it.
  7. Tap Done when it feels right. Apps you add later go last.
  8. Changed your mind? Rearrange again, then tap Back to the usual order and Done.

Your home screen, in the order you'd like.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
